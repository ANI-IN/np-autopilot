# 10-minute test script for NP team members (after Phase 1)

For 2–3 people from the NP team, on the DEV preview (access through the shareable
link; sign in with your IK Google account). The facilitator watches and notes where
people hesitate; do not help unless someone is stuck for more than a minute.

## Tasks (about 2 minutes each)

1. **Find material on a topic.** "Find existing material we have on *consistent hashing*
   (or a topic you know) and open one source." *Watch:* do they use search (Ctrl K) or
   browse? Do they find the source link?
2. **See a program's structure.** "Show me what a program is made of: its courses and
   modules." *Watch:* where do they start (Programs, Outline, Graph, the map)?
3. **See who teaches what.** "Who teaches the *System Design* material?" *Watch:* do
   they find "Who teaches what"?
4. **Move between the map and the workspace** (admins only). "Go from the map to the
   workspace graph and back." *Watch:* do they notice the top buttons?
5. **Fix something** (editors/admins). "One record has no owner. Find it and fix it."
   *Watch:* do they find Data quality and the owner field?

## Feedback form (1 minute, copy into a Google Form)

1. Which task was hardest? (1–5, one choice)
2. Where did you get stuck or confused? (free text)
3. Was any word or label unclear? Which? (free text)
4. How fast did the pages feel? (Fast / OK / Slow)
5. What would you look for here that you couldn't find? (free text)
6. Would you use this instead of searching Drive/Confluence directly? (Yes / Maybe / No, why)

Report back: task completion (yes / with help / no) per person, time per task, and
the answers. Names stay out of the report.
